The Method to the Madness

We build every space twice.

Once in the computer — to fully vet structure, finishes, and views, and to bring complete understanding to every line item. Then once again in the real world. By the time we break ground, the home has already been walked, approved, and value-engineered. Surprises don't survive the model.

"PMM allows you to experience your home
before we even break ground."
01

Connect

Architects, engineers, interior designers, builders, and clients meet inside one shared 3D model. Every discipline contributes; every decision is visible. The model becomes a common language — no decoding 2D drawings, no "trust me" moments.

02

Create

Real-time design sessions where clients drive. Materials swapped in seconds, views previewed from every window, fixtures placed and priced before a wall goes up. Anything you can imagine, we can draw and specify — in minutes, not hours.

03

Construct

The model becomes the construction document. Subs see exactly what their finished work should look like. Quantities are precise. Costs are calculated. Bi-weekly transparent budget reports keep clients in control of every dollar — to the very day.

The Pipeline

From Revit to Buildertrend, without the re-entry.

For a decade we ran the PMM process across four pieces of software that didn't talk to each other. Designers re-keyed selections from SketchUp into spreadsheets; project managers re-keyed those into Buildertrend; subs got the wrong version. PMM Tools is the bridge. Built in 2026 with AI assistance, it eliminates manual data entry across every handoff in our existing workflow — without forcing anyone to change the software they already use.

The handoffs are the product.

For ten years, the PMM process worked because our team made it work — manually carrying data across software that wouldn't speak to each other. In 2026, we partnered with AI to build the missing bridges: PMM Connection tags components automatically. PMM Horizon pushes the model to the Sheet on every save. PMM Relay auto-syncs to Buildertrend in the background. The process stayed; the manual labor disappeared.
